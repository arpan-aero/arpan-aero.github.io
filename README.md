Engineering Portfolio

Welcome to my engineering portfolio! I'm an Aeronautical Engineering student with an interest in aircraft systems, aerospace technology, mechanical design, and practical engineering.

This repository brings together projects I've worked on during my degree, covering engineering analysis, CAD modelling, product design, manufacturing, and experimental investigations. I'll continue adding projects as I develop my technical knowledge and practical experience.

Projects

1. DC Circuit Analysis and Energy Security

Area: Electrical and Electronic Engineering

Analysis of DC circuits using Ohm's Law and Kirchhoff's Laws, with NI Multisim simulations to check theoretical calculations. The project also explores renewable energy, energy security, and challenges associated with the global energy transition.

Skills: Circuit analysis, electrical calculations, NI Multisim, technical research.

2. Boeing 737 MAX MCAS and Accident Analysis
   
Area: Aerospace Engineering and Aircraft Systems

A technical study of the Boeing 737 MAX Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS), examining its operation, aircraft design considerations, and system-related issues. The report also investigates the Lion Air Flight 610 and Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302 accidents, with a focus on engineering design, system safety, and human factors.

Skills: Aircraft systems, flight control principles, technical analysis, safety investigation.

3. Mini Tape Dispenser — Design and Manufacturing

Area: Mechanical Design and Manufacturing

Design and manufacture of a functional mini tape dispenser prototype. The project involved developing and comparing design concepts, selecting a final design using a Pugh matrix, creating CAD models and technical drawings, and manufacturing components using CNC lathe machining, laser cutting, and workshop processes.

Skills: CAD modelling, design optimisation, CNC machining, laser cutting, prototyping, assembly.

4. Fluid Properties Laboratory Investigation
   
Area: Engineering Thermofluids
An experimental investigation into the density and viscosity of different fluids, including water, washing liquid, olive oil, and golden syrup. A hydrometer and falling-ball viscometer were used to collect measurements and investigate how fluid properties affect flow behaviour.

Skills: Laboratory testing, experimental measurements, fluid mechanics, data analysis, technical reporting.
